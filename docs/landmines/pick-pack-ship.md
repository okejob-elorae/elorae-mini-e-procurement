# Pick-pack-ship landmines

The full entries behind the **Pick-pack-ship** triggers in `AGENTS.md` § Landmine index, in the same order. Each one was written after someone paid for it. Read the entry for whatever surface you are about to touch before writing code; where an entry points on to `docs/ARCHITECTURE-NOTES.md`, `docs/PICK-PACK-SHIP.md` or `docs/FOLLOWUPS.md`, follow it. When a change makes an entry wrong, fix it here in the same session — and update its trigger in `AGENTS.md` if the trigger itself moved.

- Showing a `PackingVideo`'s time or recorder, or sorting/filtering packing videos by when they were recorded? A re-record overwrites the clip IN PLACE (`apps/web/lib/packer/mutations.ts`): `recordedAt` and `recordedById` stay on the FIRST take, and `replacedAt`/`updatedById` are stamped instead. So the clip that actually plays was taken at `replacedAt ?? recordedAt`, by `updatedBy` when `replacedAt` is set and `recordedBy` otherwise — the switch is on `replacedAt`, not a per-field fallback. For DISPLAY, go through `currentClipRecording` / `packingVideoRecordingOf` in `apps/web/lib/packer/clip-recording.ts`; the raw column looks right on every clip that was never re-recorded, which is why it survives review, and shows the wrong time and person on every one that was. That helper only works on rows already fetched: a query-side `orderBy` or date `where` meaning "when the current clip was recorded" must use `replacedAt ?? recordedAt` in the query itself (only `recordedAt` is indexed), never `orderBy: { recordedAt }` followed by an in-memory re-sort, which silently returns the wrong page. Raw `recordedAt` is correct only when you genuinely mean the first take — say so at the call site. Both times are UPLOAD times, not a timestamp burnt into the video (that was tried and removed: it made downloads choppy). The orphaned `app/packer/PackerListClient.tsx` still displays raw `recordedAt`, and `listPackingVideos` sorts on it; both are logged in `docs/FOLLOWUPS.md`, not a precedent. See `docs/ARCHITECTURE-NOTES.md` and `docs/PICK-PACK-SHIP.md` § Operator surfaces (PR #339).
