# Konsi sell-through report landmines

The full entries behind the **Konsi sell-through report** triggers in `AGENTS.md` § Landmine index, in the same order. Each one was written after someone paid for it. Read the entry for whatever surface you are about to touch before writing code; where an entry points on to `docs/ARCHITECTURE-NOTES.md`, `docs/EPIC-STATUS.md` or `docs/FOLLOWUPS.md`, follow it. When a change makes an entry wrong, fix it here in the same session — and update its trigger in `AGENTS.md` if the trigger itself moved.

- Touching how a sell-through report picks its ledger window (`apps/web/lib/konsi-sell-through/window.ts`)? Every `STORE` ledger row of the store must fall in EXACTLY ONE report: the window is `(previous boundary, boundary]`, store-scoped, lower edge exclusive and pinned by a test. The boundary is the MAX `createdAt` of the closing stocktake's OWN `StoreStocktake` ledger rows, so it is defined by the rows it must contain and cannot split one approval's rows across two reports — never swap in the stocktake's `approvedAt`, a separate timestamp whose order against those rows depends on write order inside the stocktake writer's transaction. `approvedAt` is only the fallback for a count that changed nothing and so wrote no rows (`setStoreStock` short-circuits). Neither is a commit time: a row's `createdAt` is fixed when its insert runs — Prisma 7 even builds `@default(now())` in the client, one `new Date()` per query — so a transaction still open when a boundary is read can later commit a row stamped at or before it (the race logged in `docs/FOLLOWUPS.md`). A database-side default would not close that gap. The race is closed ONE level deep by carry-forward: `computeLateMovements` (`apps/web/lib/konsi-sell-through/late.ts`) re-derives the PREVIOUS report's window from that report's own inputs and compares the result against that report's stored figures MINUS its stored `late*Qty` columns — what it had itself carried from ITS predecessor. The non-zero per-key differences feed the current derivation as `lateMovements`, each line that received one is flagged `hasLateMovements`, and such a line is kept even when its totals net to zero, since dropping it would lose the late figures and the next report would carry them again. Never compare against the bare totals: every report would then re-bill, negated, whatever its predecessor carried. The previous report's lines and invoice are never modified. A row that commits after TWO reports are approved is caught by neither (logged). A closing count whose boundary precedes the store's earliest `STORE` ledger row is refused `BEFORE_LEDGER_CUTOVER`: its window has no history behind it. Openings are the previous APPROVED report's line `closingQty`, never a scan for the last `balanceQty`, so each report opens exactly where the last one closed. See `docs/ARCHITECTURE-NOTES.md`.
- Cancelling a sell-through report, or adding any path that retires one? `stocktakeKey` (the closing stocktake's id) and `chainKey` (`${storeId}:${previousId ?? "root"}`) are the LIVE uniqueness keys, non-null only while the report is live (`DRAFT` or `APPROVED`) — one live report per count and one live successor per report, which is what makes one-DRAFT-per-store structural under concurrent creates rather than a pre-check. TWO retire paths exist and each nulls BOTH in its own status CAS: `cancelSellThrough` (`DRAFT` → `CANCELLED`) and `voidSellThrough` (`APPROVED` → `VOIDED`, the void entry below); a retire path that nulls only one strands either the stocktake or the store's chain slot for good, with no screen that can free it. `closingStocktakeId` and `previousId` stay behind as history, and neither is a relation — plain ids (only `closingStocktakeId` is indexed), so there is no 1:1 delete emulation to order a teardown around. See `docs/ARCHITECTURE-NOTES.md`.
- Adding a store-side `StoreStock` writer, or giving one a new `refType`? `deriveSellThroughLines` (`apps/web/lib/konsi-sell-through/derive.ts`) classifies every `STORE` ledger row in the window through an explicit `refType` switch and REFUSES anything it does not name, surfacing as `UNKNOWN_REF_TYPE` — deliberately, since the registry is exhaustive over the union and not over the column, and a skipped movement would misstate a billed quantity. Nothing compiles against that switch, so the new writer ships green and then every sell-through report at a store it touched refuses to create or approve. Decide whether the movement is in, out, sold or gap and add its case in the same change. See `docs/ARCHITECTURE-NOTES.md`.
- Setting `Store.sellThroughMethod`? KONSI-only, refused at the write boundary (`assertValidSellThroughMethod` in `apps/web/lib/stores/queries.ts`) — including a KONSI → PUTUS edit still carrying one: the writer refuses and never clears it silently, and the store form normalises it on submit the way it does `priceDiscountPercent`. A report snapshots the method at creation into `KonsiSellThrough.method`, and the hold, the resolution rules and approve all read THAT, never the store's current setting — re-reading the store would let an edit made while a report is DRAFT flip its held lines billable. `updateStore` also refuses KONSI → PUTUS while the store has a DRAFT report (`StoreHasDraftSellThroughError`), and approve re-checks the store is still KONSI (`NOT_KONSI`) — keep both, since a PUTUS store's DRAFT could otherwise never be approved. See `docs/ARCHITECTURE-NOTES.md`.
- Approving a sell-through report, or building a screen that predicts one? **Round 4 (2026-11-02):** approve BILLS NOTHING — the dialog offers `RECORD` and `BASELINE`, `INVOICE` is refused `INVOICING_RETIRED`, and konsi bills are born only by the cycle lock (`kunciAngka`, `docs/landmines/finance-ar.md`). Where the text below calls approve the konsi invoice path, read it as the retired stack kept for old rows (removal proof queries in `docs/FOLLOWUPS.md`). Approve RE-DERIVES every line inside its own transaction and refuses `STALE` on any difference from the stored figures; the remedy is cancel and recreate, never an in-place refresh, so an approved report always shows what the admin reviewed. It re-checks `NOT_KONSI`, `RETUR_IN_FLIGHT` and `TRANSFER_IN_FLIGHT` before that and `HELD` only after it, and its re-derivation includes the carry-forward, whose late figures are part of the `STALE` comparison — never move the hold ahead of the re-derivation, or an admin resolves lines on a report that must be cancelled anyway. It moves NO stock (the stocktakes already SET the balance) — do not add a mover to it. It does write one thing beyond the report: in both modes, right after the status CAS and inside the same transaction, the SPG commission split snapshot (`KonsiSellThroughSpgShare` rows and `spgSplitSnapshotAt` — history only since 2026-11-01, feeding no commission figure; the SPG commission entry near the end of this file), computed from the inputs the `STALE` check has just proved; a throw there aborts the approval, deliberately, and a refused approve writes none. It DOES move money: approving IS invoicing, and it is the only invoice path for a konsi store — never add a second one. Voiding (the next entry) is the one path that UNDOES an invoice; it creates none, so the rule stands. `INVOICE` mode prices every line, stamps the invoice date, the due date (invoice date + `Store.paymentTempo` days), the total and the salesman on the report and `unitPrice`/`lineTotal` on each line, and creates the `Receivable` and `TaxInvoice` only when the total is above zero. Right after `UNPRICED`, `INVOICE` refuses `PRICE_CHANGED` (detail: the live total) when the live total differs to the cent from `expectedTotal`, the total the dialog showed, which `parseApproveRequest` requires on every screen approve; the remedy is to refresh and approve again, with no cancel. It compares the TOTAL only, and the writer skips it when `expectedTotal` is undefined, which only internal and fixture callers do — a new screen caller must send it. Each line's `unitCost` is snapshotted at creation from the store's `StoreStock.avgCost`, falling back to main's `InventoryValue.avgCost` (through `findExistingInventoryValueRow`) when that is missing or 0; a line still at 0 is only warned about in the dialog (`uncostedKeys`) — never turn that into a refusal, since no screen can set a store's average cost and approve would wedge. `BASELINE` mode is for a store's FIRST report only (`previousId` null, else `BASELINE_NOT_FIRST`), needs a reason and bills nothing — no receivable, faktur or journal. That is true of the MODE and not of the `baseline` column: the migration marked every report already `APPROVED` before invoicing existed as a baseline, first or not, and their billed units were not necessarily billed by hand — so never read `baseline: true` as "first report" or as "invoiced outside the ERP". The revenue, COGS, shrinkage and surplus journals (`KONSI_SELLTHRU_REVENUE`/`_COGS`/`_SHRINKAGE`/`_SURPLUS`, all dated on the invoice date) are posted by `approveSellThroughAction` AFTER the writer commits, each through `postArJournalSafely`, and one still owed is retried from the report's own page, never the piutang detail. COGS and shrinkage credit, and surplus debits, `INVENTORY_CONSIGNMENT` (store-tagged; COGS also carries channel KONSINYASI), never the warehouse `INVENTORY`: the goods were debited there when their delivery shipment completed (`CONSIGNMENT_TRANSFER`, one journal per `KonsiTransfer`, `postJurnalPindahKonsi` in `apps/web/lib/finance/operasional/jurnal.ts`). The role must be mapped in Account Mapping before a konsi store's first approve; unmapped, each journal becomes `JOURNAL_PENDING` (`UNMAPPED_ROLE`) and stays owed on the report page. Never switch them back to `INVENTORY`: that drives the warehouse account negative while `INVENTORY_CONSIGNMENT` only grows. Reports journaled before the switch credited `INVENTORY`, and their voids mirror that. The surplus journal (Dr `INVENTORY_CONSIGNMENT` / Cr `INVENTORY_VARIANCE`, Σ `surplusQty` × `unitCost`) values each line's STORED `surplusQty`, which only an `INVOICE` approve writes (`unabsorbedSurplusQty`, `derive.ts`); a `BASELINE` approve leaves it 0, and so does every report approved before the column existed, which is what keeps the rule non-retroactive — never backfill the column, and never derive surplus from a line's other figures at post time, or every older report reads as owing a journal. The retry and the page's pending banner are gated on the owed journal being MISSING (`sellThroughJournalGaps`, `journal.ts`), not on a `JOURNAL_PENDING` flag — a flag never clears, so the banner would outlive a successful retry, and a crash between the commit and the posts writes none, so no retry would ever appear. That gate is safe here only because every report approved before invoicing existed is a baseline and owes nothing; never copy it onto the delivery retry, whose backfilled receivables have no journal by construction. Its rules have one spelling each: the create preconditions are `checkSellThroughPreconditions` (`writer.ts`), run by both `createSellThrough` and the read-only `getSellThroughEligibility`; the gap-sign → resolution arms and the needs-a-reason rule are `resolutionArmsFor`/`resolutionNeedsReason` (`derive.ts`), read by the writer and the detail screen; the price is `priceSellThroughLines` (`pricing.ts`), run by approve and by the DRAFT preview; and who may own the receivable is `SALESMAN_CANDIDATE_WHERE` (`salesman-candidates.ts`), read by the dialog's list and the writer's check. Never restate any of them beside a caller. See `docs/ARCHITECTURE-NOTES.md`.
- Voiding a sell-through report, or adding a path that retires an approved one? `voidSellThrough` (`apps/web/lib/konsi-sell-through/void-writer.ts`) is the only way out of `APPROVED`, invoiced or baseline, and it exists so a corrected report can be created from the same count. Its refusals run in a fixed order: the reason first, before the transaction opens (`VOID_REASON_REQUIRED`, with detail `REASON_TOO_LONG` over 1000 characters); then `NOT_FOUND`; then the status CAS, which refuses anything not `APPROVED` as `INVALID_STATE` — there is no `NOT_APPROVED` code — and makes a double void, or a void racing an approve, structurally impossible; then `HAS_SUCCESSOR` (detail: the later report's docNo) while a `DRAFT` or `APPROVED` report names this one as its previous, because the next report opens from this one's closing figures — so only a store's latest live report can be voided and a chain unwinds from its tail. That successor is found by a `findUnique` on `chainKey` = `${storeId}:${id}`, never a query on `previousId`: `chainKey` is set only while a report is live and cancel and void both null it, so the two agree on every report the check is looking for, and under SERIALIZABLE the `@unique` point lookup locks only at that one key where a query on the unindexed `previousId` locks broadly — never put it back on `previousId`; then, on the receivable, `ALREADY_SETTLED` (`WRITTEN_OFF`), `HAS_PAYMENTS` (`paidAmount > 0` — void each payment first; a component of an `APPROVED` store settlement cannot be voided, `SETTLEMENT_COMPONENT` in `docs/landmines/finance-ar.md`, so a report whose invoice an approved settlement paid cannot be voided at all), `SETTLEMENT_PENDING` (detail: the settlement's docNo) and `COLLECTION_PENDING`. Every refusal from `NOT_FOUND` on throws inside the transaction, so one raised after the CAS rolls it back. In one serializable transaction it flips the report to `VOIDED` and nulls BOTH live keys exactly as `cancelSellThrough` does (the retire entry above), sets the receivable `VOIDED` with `outstandingAmount` 0 (`originalAmount` kept), sets the faktur `CANCELLED` from whatever status it had reached — directly, not through the faktur writer's `transition`, so no `TAX_INVOICE_*` audit row is written for it — and writes one `KONSI_SELL_THROUGH_VOID` audit row whose reason is capped at 191 code points (the full reason stays in `voidReason`). It moves no stock. `voidSellThroughAction` never reports failure once the writer has returned: the gaps read and the reversal posts run in their own try/catch that only logs, with the revalidation in its `finally`, so a throw after commit returns `{ ok: true }` and the reversal it left reads as owed on the page (`approveSellThroughAction` takes the same shape). It posts the reversals after commit, each through `postArJournalSafely`: `postSellThroughVoidJournal` (`journal.ts`) MIRRORS the posted original — every line's debit and credit swapped on the same chart account, deliberately bypassing `resolveAccount` so a mapping changed since approve cannot send the reversal to a different account than the original hit — dated on the VOID (the report's `voidedAt`), never on the original's date, so a void never moves an earlier period finance may already have reported; never before the original either (the later of the two wins), and on the original's date if `voidedAt` is missing, never the clock, so a retry dates it as the first attempt would have — under `KONSI_SELLTHRU_REVENUE_VOID`/`_COGS_VOID`/`_SHRINKAGE_VOID`/`_SURPLUS_VOID`, so both entries stand in the GL. Never recompute a reversal from the report's figures and never route it through `generateAutoJournal`. The owed set, the retry and the page's banner all read `sellThroughJournalGaps`, which for a `VOIDED` report owes reversals ONLY, each exactly when its original exists and it does not: an original that never posted owes no reversal, and an approve's original post landing after the void then reads as an owed reversal. A reversal cannot fail `UNMAPPED_ROLE`; its realistic failure is a thrown `NON_POSTABLE_ACCOUNT` (an original's account deactivated or given children since approve), and for the void kinds `postArJournalSafely`'s flag says to reactivate the original account or post the reversal by manual journal, never "Map the account" — a mirror posts to the original's accounts, so no mapping change can fix it (PR #474). Approve refuses `INVALID_STATE` (detail `PREVIOUS_REPORT`) when a DRAFT's previous report is no longer `APPROVED` — a guard behind `HAS_SUCCESSOR`, not a path. `recordSellThroughNotaPrinted`'s first-print CAS also requires the report to be `APPROVED`, so a nota print that began before a void and lands after it is a silent no-op like a reprint: it stamps nothing on the `CANCELLED` faktur and sends finance no `TAX_INVOICE_PENDING`. Recreating is the ordinary create from the same count: nothing links the new report to the voided one, and its `previousId` is the store's latest `APPROVED` report — the voided one's own predecessor, or none. The void codes are raised only from the report page, so they carry copy under `konsiSellThrough.err.*` alone; `HAS_SUCCESSOR` and `SETTLEMENT_PENDING` interpolate their docNo through their own branch in `errorMessage` (`SellThroughDetailClient.tsx`). See `docs/ARCHITECTURE-NOTES.md`.
- Adding a `SellThroughErrorCode`? The code is passed to the screen as-is with no exhaustive `Record`, so nothing fails to compile — a missing string renders the raw key. A code the CREATE path can raise needs copy in BOTH locales under TWO namespaces: `konsiSellThrough.err.<CODE>` for the report's own screens, and `storeStocktakes.detail.sellThrough.reason.<CODE>` for the create button on the stocktake detail page, which renders precondition refusals and create failures alike. The second is the one that gets missed — it already was once, caught in review.
- Adding a sell-through approve refusal? It needs `konsiSellThrough.err.<CODE>` in BOTH locales. The invoicing codes (`UNPRICED`, `PRICE_CHANGED`, `INVALID_INVOICE_DATE`, `SALESMAN_REQUIRED`, `SALESMAN_INVALID`, `BASELINE_NOT_FIRST`, `BASELINE_REASON_REQUIRED`) have NO key under `storeStocktakes…` on purpose — the stocktake page creates reports and never approves one, and only approve raises them; do not add one to complete a pair. That does not extend to every code approve can raise: `HELD` and `STALE` do carry keys there. A code whose `detail` carries data rather than copy also needs its own branch in `errorMessage` (`SellThroughDetailClient.tsx`), whose generic arm interpolates `detail` raw: `UNPRICED`'s detail is the refused lines' comma-joined `itemId::variantSku` keys, named back as products through `productNamesForKeys`, and `PRICE_CHANGED`'s is the live total, formatted as rupiah in its own branch.
- Pricing a sell-through line? The invoice bills the item's CATALOG selling price. `priceSellThroughLines` (`apps/web/lib/konsi-sell-through/pricing.ts`) does not call `computeStorePrice` and never reads `Store.markupPercent`: the markup prices what the store's own customer pays at the SPG POS, and the store keeps the markup, so it never reaches the invoice — and a KONSI store with no markup still invoices. A line is UNPRICED only when its item has no usable selling price; approve refuses `UNPRICED` naming every billed line it could not price, and the remedy is setting the selling price and approving again — approve and the DRAFT preview both reprice live, so unlike `STALE` no cancel is needed. Never route the invoice back through `computeStorePrice`'s KONSI branch: it would bill the store its customer's marked-up price. See `docs/ARCHITECTURE-NOTES.md`.
- Creating a sell-through report, or changing how returns settle at a KONSI store? A retur takes its goods off the shelf when raised but `StoreStock` drops only later — at approve for a FIELD retur, at receipt (plus an approve-time delta) for an ADMIN one — so `RETUR_IN_FLIGHT` refuses a closing count when any retur of the store (either origin) was raised on or before the count moment (`countFinishedAt ?? approvedAt`) and is still pending OR was approved only after that moment. Never narrow it to the pending statuses: approving the retur later does not clean a count that missed those units. The remedy is to finish or cancel the returs, then close the period with a later count, whose rows net the contaminated one out. Cancelling is a real option only for a retur not yet received: `cancelFieldReturn` (`apps/web/lib/field-sales/retur/cancel-writer.ts`) accepts `PENDING_WAREHOUSE_RECEIVING` alone, and a CANCELLED retur refuses neither this check nor the stocktake's `RETUR_PENDING`; a received one can only be settled through approval. Since stocktake approval refuses an open retur on counted keys, a count usually reaches this check through the approved-later arm. `TRANSFER_IN_FLIGHT` (`assertNoTransferInFlight`, beside it, run from the same two places) refuses while a PENDING store transfer touching the store — from it or to it — and moving an item::variant the closing count COUNTED has `movedAt` ≤ the count moment. It deliberately has NO approved-later arm: an approved transfer's ledger rows sit inside the window and the stocktake exclusion kept `StoreStock` right, so the report derives the move as in/out with no gap. Never copy the retur's `approvedAt > countMoment` arm across. Its remedy differs from the retur's too: such a transfer can no longer be approved (`COUNTED_SINCE_MOVE`), so it is cancelled, the move stands in the count as variance, and it is raised again for any keys the count did not count, or with the real move time if the goods moved after the count. Its copy lives in both namespaces, like every create-path `SellThroughErrorCode`. See `docs/ARCHITECTURE-NOTES.md`.
- Touching SPG commission — Komisi SPG, the recap (`/backoffice/spg-commission`), the SPG pay screen, the consignment cycle lock, a sell-through approve/void path, or a POS return? **Round 4 (2026-11-02):** the category is the one RECORDED on the sale line (`SpgSaleLine.categoryId`/`categoryAt`, filled later only through `isiKategoriPenjualanSpg`, P69); a buyer return lowers commission in the month of the ORIGINAL nota (P9); one payment per SPG per month carries base salary + commission; putus pay still needs the report check (a month with a scheduled SPG and no sales is checked too). Every SPG commission figure, at konsi AND putus stores, comes from ONE calculation since the owner's 2026-11-01 decision (per SPG from her own sales, never an equal split — confirmed 2026-11-02): `apps/web/lib/spg-commission/payout.ts` over a CHECKED sales report (loaded by `laporan-queries.ts`) — the item CATEGORY rate in force in the SALES month (`resolveRate`, Settings → Komisi SPG) × the pieces each SPG sold HERSELF, net of her own notes' shopper returns that the office APPROVED (`returPembeliBerlaku`), counted in the month the return was REPORTED, never below 0, split by largest remainder. A return approved after its month was checked changes the recompute, and the payout corrects it (next entry); a report still MENUNGGU, rejected or withdrawn never counts. A konsi line confirms the smaller of the official locked qty and the POS pieces (`confirmedPieces`); a putus line confirms the POS pieces. A sale whose item has no category, or whose category has no rate that month, earns 0 and is reported unrated. Commission belongs to the sales month but is PAID in the WIB month the report was CHECKED — `ConsignmentCycle.lockedAt` for a konsi cycle (locking IS the check, `kunciAngka`), `SpgSalesCheck.checkedAt` for a putus store × month ("Tandai laporan dicek", only once the month is over) — and carries the note "Komisi penjualan <bulan>" (`payoutMonth`, `salesMonthNoteId`). Past months' rates are closed (`setRate` refuses one), so the check date never moves an amount. Putus (`apps/web/lib/spg/komisi-writer.ts`): setting a commission (`tetapkanKomisiToko`) needs a `SpgSalesCheck`; paying it is the next entry; checking refuses a month that confirms no piece (`TANPA_PENJUALAN`, through `confirmsAnyPiece` in `payout.ts` — the same test the "menunggu dicek" queue and the Beranda count `spgCommissionsUnset` use, so a putus month whose SPG sales were all returned by shoppers is in no queue) and a store × month that already has a paid row (`SUDAH_DIBAYAR`: the old-rule money already left, and the recap would count the month again); unchecking needs a reason, is allowed only while nothing is paid, and deletes the set rows (releasing their lost-goods deductions). Each step writes its audit row in the same transaction, and the pay guard (`paidAt = null`) runs inside `runSerializable`. Konsi re-lock keeps `ConsignmentCommission.salaried` from the first lock (`PESERTA_KUNCI_PERTAMA`), so a seller from another store (`salaried = false`) never gets base salary through a re-lock; the commission itself always follows who sold. `getCommissionRecap` (`queries.ts`, the recap page and its export) reads by PAYOUT month and shows the STORED figure as paid (`ConsignmentCommission.commission` at the cycle's lock, `SpgCommission.commission` at Tetapkan), the recomputation only beside it as "hitungan" with a per-SPG differs flag (`lib/spg-commission/recap-payout.ts`); a checked putus month with no `SpgCommission` row is `BELUM_DITETAPKAN`, never paid. Its putus months come from ONE loader shared with Komisi SPG, `putusPayoutReports` (`laporan-queries.ts`): months checked in the payout month plus old-rule months set without a check, in the payout month their rows were last set. It no longer reads konsi sell-through reports: reading both would count one month twice. The SPG home pay card reads the same official rows as `/pwa/spg/gaji`: `gajiKomisiSpg` (`apps/web/lib/spg/queries.ts`, behind `ringkasanSpg`) and the pay page both read `ringkasGajiSaya` (`apps/web/lib/spg/komisi-queries.ts`; `komisiResmiSaya` is its `.resmi`) — never a second query keyed by sales month, or the two screens name different months for one payment. Never compute a figure from `Item.commissionPerPcs` (`lib/items/komisi-spg.ts`), from an equal split (`bagiKomisi` is deleted), or from the rate copied into `ConsignmentSold.commissionPerPcs` at lock — that column now records the category rate used, as history. The sell-through approve still writes `KonsiSellThroughSpgShare` rows and stamps `spgSplitSnapshotAt` (`split-weights.ts`), but they feed no figure: never write or edit them outside approve, and never build a reader that turns them into a second commission source. The leftovers (unused settings and helpers, export columns) are in `docs/FOLLOWUPS.md` § Field Sales / PWA.
- Paying SPG commission (`tandaiKomisiDibayar` in `apps/web/lib/spg/komisi-writer.ts`, konsi `bayarKomisi` in `apps/web/lib/konsinyasi/writer.ts`), writing an `SpgCommissionCorrection`, or showing a pay figure on any screen? **Round 4 (2026-11-02):** the only holds left are a missing SALARY BASIS (salary not set, monthly schedule not approved, store check-in record missing or unchecked — P60, salary AND commission), the old-rule holds are gone, the 12-month correction window stays, charges and paybacks are required planner inputs deducted in full commission-then-salary, and a departed SPG's uncovered remainder becomes `SpgDebt` (P66–P68). Owner decision 2026-11-02: unpaid rows are PAID at the CURRENT per-SPG figure, never the stored one. The pay transaction re-plans the row (`siapkanBayarKomisi` in `apps/web/lib/spg/koreksi-writer.ts`, over the pure `rencanaBayarKomisi` in `koreksi.ts`) and, when the figure moved, rewrites `commission`/`unitsSold`/basis and writes `SPG_COMMISSION_RECOMPUTED_AT_PAYMENT` (`catatHitungUlangSaatBayar`, carrying `storeId`) in the SAME transaction — the finance sweep finds an accrual left wrong by a dead post-commit hook through exactly that audit row, so never move the update or the audit out of the `rencana.berubah` block, and never rename its action, `entityType` or `entityId`. The lost-goods deduction is kept, or released and re-cut down to the new cap whenever the plan's deduction is below the stored one (never raised). `jumlahDiharapkan` — the figure the office saw — is compared before any write; a mismatch throws `ANGKA_BERUBAH` with the current figure, and the pay dialog shows the exact amount. `ATURAN_LAMA` is gone: an old-rule row is paid at today's figure too. Putus pay needs a `SpgSalesCheck` OR an already-paid sibling in the same store × month (such a month cannot be checked any more, and its other rows must not stay locked forever). The pay guard (`paidAt = null`) runs inside `runSerializable`. CORRECTIONS: one unit = one report × one SPG; a unit is CLOSED when its row is paid, or when it has no row and its report is closed (the store × month pairs of every putus unit loaded beforehand are read, so a seller with no row of her own still sees the month's paid flag). Its difference = current figure − paid − corrections already applied from that unit, DERIVED and never stored. It rides ONCE on that SPG's oldest payable unpaid row inside `KOREKSI_JENDELA_BULAN` (12 months, `lib/spg/komisi.ts`), and is written as `SpgCommissionCorrection` (unique per unit × target, never updated or deleted) only when that row is marked paid, in that payment's transaction with its audit row. Positive corrections apply in full; negative ones, oldest sales month first, only as far as the payout stays at or above Rp 0 — the rest stays a derived difference and carries to the payout after. HOLDS: an OLD-RULE unit that cannot be compared honestly (`alasanTahan`: `TANPA_TARIF`, pieces without a category rate; `LAPORAN_TIDAK_LENGKAP`, a konsi cycle whose official figures are not all on the SPG's phone) is neither corrected nor paid — not at the old figure, not at Rp 0 — until the owner decides, and a held konsi row holds its base salary too. The same hold rule is applied in THREE places: `unitKomisiSpg` (`koreksi-queries.ts`), `alasanTahanTunggakan` (the Beranda count `spgCommissionsUnpaidPast` and the #tunggakan card) and `siapkanBayarKomisi` (the writer) — change all three or none; new-rule reports are never held. The phone never shows a stale or undecided amount: an unchecked old-rule putus month shows the figure it will be paid once checked (`pratinjauSesudahDicek`, with the old figure as "sebelumnya"), and a held row (`ditahan`) or a konsi cycle the office unlocked again (`belumPasti`) shows no amount on `/pwa/spg/gaji` or the home card. Beranda `spgCommissionsUnpaidPast` (`jumlahKomisiSpgTertunggak`) counts only payable in-window arrears; held rows and rows older than the window are listed apart on the card. Tests: a DB test that pays must delete `SpgCommissionCorrection` rows by its seeded users through `seededId()`, and one that approves shopper returns must also delete their `SpgSaleReturn` audit rows.
- Posting an SPG commission or salary payment journal (`apps/web/lib/finance/operasional/aturan.ts`, `jurnal.ts`, `tertunda.ts`, `sapuan.ts`)? It carries that payment's applied `SpgCommissionCorrection` rows: DR `SPG_COMMISSION_EXPENSE` at the SOURCE report's store and channel (KONSINYASI when `sourceKind` is `KONSI`, else PUTUS) / CR `COMMISSION_PAYABLE` (`barisKoreksiKomisi`), and it credits BANK for net + the sum of corrections, which must equal the writer's `dibayar` — so a negative correction may never drive net + corrections below zero. The corrections must commit in the SAME transaction as `paidAt`: `postSekali` never revisits a posted payment. `postingJurnalOperasionalAman` re-syncs the `KOMISI_SPG` / `KOMISI_KONSI` accrual FIRST on every path that posts a payment journal (the post-commit hook, the scheduled sweep and Posting ulang), because a pay can recompute the figure. The sweep also finds a paid row whose own net is 0 when it carries corrections that do not cancel out per store × kind. A payment recomputed to zero net with no correction has no journal at all; if its hook dies, the accrual finders catch the accrual through the `SPG_COMMISSION_RECOMPUTED_AT_PAYMENT` audit row (`akrualTertinggal`: already journaled, nothing posted after the recompute, target differs from what is posted). Corrections never get a journal of their own.
- Reading or writing an SPG shopper return (`SpgSaleReturn`/`SpgSaleReturnLine`, `SpgSaleLine.returnedQty`)? **Round 4 (2026-11-02):** the column default is `MENUNGGU`; commission readers date an approved return by its ORIGINAL nota (P9), net sales and the konsi report by the report, the closing day by the refund day; the refund is recorded only after approval (or while waiting, if the cashier already paid) with the slip photo (P77). Owner decision 2026-11-02: a shopper return at the counter is a REPORT. `catatReturPembeli` (`apps/web/lib/spg/retur-pembeli.ts`) writes a `MENUNGGU` row and moves NOTHING — store stock book, net sales, SPG commission, the konsi SPG report, Elorae promo deductions, `SalesHistory` and `SpgSaleLine.returnedQty` stay as before — until `setujuiReturPembeli` (`spg_returns:approve`, admin and ADMIN_PENJUALAN by default, never the reporter: `PELAPOR_SAMA`) applies exactly what reporting used to apply. Rejecting needs a reason sent to the SPG's phone; the reporter can withdraw a pending report (`DIBATALKAN`); all three outcomes are final. Every counting reader goes through ONE predicate, `returPembeliBerlaku` (`lib/spg/retur-pembeli-berlaku.ts`, status `DISETUJUI`, written last so a caller cannot override it), dated at the REPORT `createdAt`, never `decidedAt`; `retur-pembeli-berlaku-cakupan.test.ts` scans the sources and fails a new reader that skips it. It exists twice by design: the FUNCTION there and a CONSTANT of the same name in `retur-pembeli.ts` — never spread the function. `SpgSaleLine.returnedQty` already means approved-only (only approval raises it), so its readers need no filter; what is still pending is derived (`qtyMenungguPerBaris`) and also reduces what can still be reported from that line, so one piece is never reported twice. Every writer runs in `runSerializable`, locks the `SpgSale` row FOR UPDATE as its first statement (the decision writers read the immutable `spgSaleId` outside the transaction, so there is no S→X upgrade) and compare-and-swaps on `MENUNGGU`. Laku bersih reads an approved LAYAK line with no `RETUR_PEMBELI` store-book row pointing at its report (`refType` `"SpgSaleReturn"`, `refId` = the report id, same item × variant) as counted by a store opname (`returTanpaBukuLaku`), so fixtures must set `refType`/`refId` on that row. The column DEFAULT `DISETUJUI` is the backfill and keeps rows the old image writes in the deploy window consistent; rolling back after reports arrived makes old readers count every status — decide pending reports first. A buyer return line on a store return (NRT) is accepted only when that return is approved (`periksaBarisReturPembeli` → `RETUR_PEMBELI_TIDAK_SAH`), and the phone offers only approved ones.
- Adding a way for a sell-through report to leave `DRAFT` for `APPROVED`, or to leave `APPROVED`? Approve and void are manager decisions since 2026-11-01: `approveSellThroughAction` and `voidSellThroughAction` go through `guardDecide` (`apps/web/app/actions/konsi-sell-through.ts`), which checks `consignment:lock` (admin-only by default, `persetujuan: true`), never this module's `guard()` on `stores:manage` — approving IS invoicing the store. Create, line resolution, cancelling a DRAFT, printing and the journal retry stay on `stores:manage`. The report page passes `canDecide` for the Approve and Void buttons (and the held-lines hint beside Approve), off the same code. A new exit from either status takes `guardDecide` and the button checks `consignment:lock`; a new screen calling one of them must do the same.
- Paying an SPG, or adding a pay-plan caller (2026-11-02 round 4)? One payment per SPG per month: base salary + commission. Base salary = `SpgSalary` in force for that month ÷ scheduled working days of the APPROVED monthly schedule (`SpgMonthSchedule`), minus one daily rate per scheduled day without a check-in at her store (approved izin/sakit/cuti excepted), plus one daily rate per worked day off (P65a); for days without a schedule a CHECKED store check-in record (`SpgAttendanceRecord`) is the basis and wins over the system check-in for the days it covers (P61). Mid-month start or end: salary ÷ the working days of a full month on the standard SPG work days × her scheduled days (P62). A month with no salary basis holds salary AND commission (P60) and its reason links to the screen that fixes it. Charges (lost goods, refused buyer returns) and paybacks are REQUIRED inputs of every plan caller (`bebanSpgTerbukaPerOrang`, `kembaliBebanTerbukaPerOrang`), allocated LAST (after corrections) on what is left of commission and then salary; with several unpaid rows `bagiBebanKeBaris` splits them per row. A departed SPG's final payment carries every correction; what does not fit becomes `SpgDebt`, and a later plus first reduces the debt, the rest paid as `SpgExtraPayout` (P67); debt payments wait for the bank check by someone other than the recorder (`payments:verify`, P68/decision 11). Journals: CR `BANK` = what was actually paid (`dibayarkanTersimpan`).
- Entering or checking the SPG store check-in record (`apps/web/lib/spg/absen-catatan-hadir*.ts`, `/backoffice/komisi-spg/catatan-hadir`)? The office enters it — typed per day or uploaded (Excel/CSV template: Tanggal, Jadwal KERJA/LIBUR, Hadir YA/TIDAK, Catatan) — under `spg_attendance:record` (admin and Admin Penjualan by default, P7e), and Admin Keuangan checks it against the paper under `spg_attendance:verify` (P61a); the checker can never be the person who entered it. Only a DIPERIKSA record counts; DIAJUKAN and DIKEMBALIKAN records are ignored and hold the month. A paid month's record cannot change. An out-of-radius check-in counts as present and is flagged for review, never blocked (P70). The page guards itself (`bolehMembukaCatatanHadir`) — it is not in `ROUTE_PERMISSIONS`.
- Refunding an SPG buyer return (`apps/web/lib/spg/retur-pembeli*.ts`, `beban-spg*.ts`)? The SPG reports first with a mandatory goods photo and sets the goods aside; the cashier refunds only AFTER approval, recorded with the slip photo (P13a/P77) — or, if the cashier already paid while the report waited, the SPG answers "Sudah" with the slip. The refund step refuses a DITOLAK/DIBATALKAN report, offline captures included (`TIDAK_DISETUJUI`, terminal). A report with a recorded refund is never withdrawable. Rejected after a refund becomes an `SpgCharge` at the net nota price, or the slip amount the office sets with a reason (P78/P81); it is deducted commission-then-salary, never waived; once paid the goods wait for a decision (KEMBALI_STOK with the money back on the next payment, or DIBELI_SPG, P79). Reversing a wrong rejection (P82) is refused once anything was deducted or the charge moved to `SpgDebt` (`SUDAH_DIUTANGKAN`).
